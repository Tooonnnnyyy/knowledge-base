## English

Collaborative filtering learns item affinity from a matrix of users and items with observed interactions. Matrix factorization and nearest-neighbor methods infer missing preferences from patterns across users or items.

### A distinct extension

A synthetic baseline can use explicit latent preference vectors and random exposure. That is a different mechanism from collaborative filtering. MovieLens or Amazon ratings could support a collaborative-filtering extension; inverse-transform sampling and traffic processes would remain separate simulation mechanisms.

## 中文

Collaborative filtering 从用户与物品的已观测交互矩阵中学习偏好。矩阵分解和近邻方法利用用户之间或物品之间的模式，推断尚未观察到的偏好。

### 一种独立的扩展

合成基线可以使用显式的潜在偏好向量和随机曝光，这与协同过滤属于不同机制。MovieLens 或 Amazon ratings 可以支持协同过滤扩展；inverse-transform sampling 和流量过程仍应作为独立的仿真机制处理。
