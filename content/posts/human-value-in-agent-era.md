## English

### The scarce part of work moves upstream

AI can amplify differences in judgment, taste, supervision and direction. As tools become widely available, the distinction is no longer only whether someone can execute a task.

A person still has to notice when the direction is wrong, recognize a structural problem and redefine the question before execution begins.

### Three observations

1. An AI system often starts by following the first direction it receives.
2. Judgment about direction, structure and priorities remains scarce.
3. Human value therefore includes critical thinking, supervision, authority, taste and strategic decision-making.

When execution becomes cheaper, deciding what deserves execution can become more valuable.

## 中文

### 核心判断

AI 不只是替代机械劳动，它会放大人与人之间在判断、品味、监督、方向选择上的差距。

### 关键含义

- 工具普及以后，壁垒不再只是“会不会做”
- 更大的差异来自：
  - 是否能看出方向本身错了
  - 是否能识别底层架构问题
  - 是否能在执行前重定义问题

### 这条思路的三个判断

1. AI 往往先沿着用户给的第一方向执行。
2. 真正稀缺的是对方向、结构、优先级的判断。
3. 所以人的价值更偏向：
   - critical thinking
   - supervision
   - authority
   - taste
   - strategic decision-making

### 与 agent 时代的关系

当执行能力变便宜，判断能力会更贵。
