## English

Synthetic data is generated by a stated model rather than collected from a real person. A synthetic user has latent attributes and simulated responses. It is useful for controlled tests of a mechanism, but it cannot establish real-world preferences or causal effects.

### A transparent example

A user receives a Dirichlet preference vector, random content exposures, sampled watch times, actions and churn outcomes. The seed and parameters make the experiment reproducible. Its usefulness comes from making the assumptions inspectable and changeable.

## 中文

合成数据由明确的模型生成，不是从真实个人身上采集的。合成用户拥有潜在属性和被模拟的反应，适合用于可控的机制测试，但不能建立真实世界的偏好或因果效果。

### 一个透明的例子

可以为用户生成 Dirichlet 偏好向量、随机内容曝光、抽样的观看时长、动作和流失结果。Seed 与参数让实验可复现，价值在于假设可以被检查和修改。
